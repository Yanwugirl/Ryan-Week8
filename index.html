About

Material Shadow starts from the universal charger I took apart for Project 0. Each click opens one layer of what the object depends on: its parts, the materials inside them, the places those materials come from, and what is left behind there.

The work combines two strategies from this week's session. Like Infrastructure of a Migratory Bird, it maps a network instead of a single object. Like Rare Earthenware, it gives the hidden cost a felt form: the more you open, the heavier your cursor becomes. It lags behind your hand and sinks, so even clicking gets harder.

The weight is symbolic. It grows with each layer you open, not with real tonnage. The facts in the text come from the sources listed below, and each node shows its own source. The charger itself is small; most of what it costs is somewhere else.
